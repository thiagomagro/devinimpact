AI-DLC with the impact of Devin

The phases of the AI-Driven Development Life Cycle (AWS) and how Devin implements each one with Knowledge, Playbooks, Skills, Plugins, Automations and DeepWiki.

Thesis: AI-DLC organizes AI-driven development into short phases with human approval gates, persistent memory and continuous learning. Devin implements this cycle with native primitives: Knowledge is the memory, Playbooks are the gated workflows, Skills are the versioned procedures, Plugins are the integrations, Automations are the recurring triggers and DeepWiki is the living map of the system.

Each card shows one stage of the cycle, what changes with Devin and which mechanisms support the stage. The approval gates and the decision owners remain human.

Approval gates Where the human decides, with an audit trail

Gates are not bureaucracy — they are the control mechanism. In AI-DLC, each phase ends in a human checkpoint. In Devin, gates become explicit playbook steps and auditable approvals (approve_deploy, approve_test_app, human PR review). Autonomy is a dial per stage, not a global switch.

Based on the AI-DLC (AI-Driven Development Life Cycle) published by AWS: article on AWS Builder Center and reference implementation (aidlc-workflows).

The reference AI-DLC uses 14 agents, 32 stages, 9 scopes and 3 depth levels. The mapping here shows how the same concepts are implemented with Devin's native primitives, without replacing the pipeline or the decision owners.
